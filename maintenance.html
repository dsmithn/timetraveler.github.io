What we do on every visit

  1. Wash or replace customer-supplied filters
  2. Test safety control(s)
  3. Clear drain line(s)
  4. Clean drain pan (all accessible areas)
  5. Treat drain line(s) and pan
  6. Check temperature splits
  7. Inspect evaporator coil
  8. Test thermostat operation
  9. Check heat operation
  10. Inspect electrical connections
  11. Check electrical amps and voltages
  12. Test capacitor(s)
  13. Check refrigerant pressures
  14. Rinse condenser coil (if water is available)
